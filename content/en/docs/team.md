# Team

$mol is built in the open by **[hyoo-ru](https://github.com/hyoo-ru)** — the community around its author, Dmitry Karlovsky ([nin-jin](https://github.com/nin-jin)). Development happens in a single monorepo, [mam_mol](https://github.com/hyoo-ru/mam_mol), where the framework, its components, and the tooling all live together.

The ecosystem is a group effort: the core framework, the [$hyoo_crowd](https://github.com/hyoo-ru/crowd.hyoo.ru) CRDT library, [Giper Baza](https://github.com/giper-dev/baza), and dozens of published components all come from contributors working in the same workspace.

## Contributing

- The whole ecosystem is MIT-licensed and open to pull requests.
- Every module lives in the [mam_mol](https://github.com/hyoo-ru/mam_mol) monorepo — fork, add a folder, open a PR.
- Discuss ideas and share what you build in the [DEV community](https://dev.to/t/mol).

This documentation site is maintained separately at [b-on-g/smalljs](https://github.com/b-on-g/smalljs); every page has an *Edit on GitHub* link if you spot something to improve.
